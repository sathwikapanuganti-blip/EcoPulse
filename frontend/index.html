What is EcoPulse and Why Does It Exist?

A friendly community platform to protect the air we breathe, the water we drink, and the food we share.

1. Know Your Air & Weather

Instantly check if it's safe to play outdoors, go for a morning walk, or if you should wear a mask.

2. Stop Waste in Your Neighborhood

Spot a leaking tap, overflowing tank, or extra food after an event? Report it in 10 seconds so help arrives.

3. Build Greener Habits Together

Track your personal eco points and help your apartment, school, or office become the greenest community.

🚨 Report an Environmental Problem

Notify your neighborhood wardens of garbage dumping, plastic pollution, smoke, or overflowing bins.

🍲 Food Waste Mitigation & Surplus Sharing

Report extra food from marriages, canteens, or homes for quick redistribution to shelters.

💧 Report Clean Water Leakage or Wastage

Every drop saved counts! Help maintenance teams fix leaks before gallons are lost.

🌱 Log Your Green Action

Every positive step boosts your personal Eco Impact score and earns community badges!

Litres for water, kg for food, items for plastic/recycling, trips for transit.
